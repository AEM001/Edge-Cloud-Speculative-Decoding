What the async overlap is doing
Sync:  [draft 35ms][wait+verify 110ms] = 145ms/round
Async: [draft 35ms] starts immediately after submitting;
       [wait for prev verify 75ms bubble] then submit = 110ms/round
That's a 24% per-round speedup from async alone — the numbers (async avg_rtt drops from 108ms → 110ms reported but actual wall time is lower due to overlap). The absolute gain is real but modest because the RTT dominates.
